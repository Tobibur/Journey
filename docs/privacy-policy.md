# Privacy Policy — Journey

**Last updated: 17 June 2026**

Journey ("the app") is a personal journaling application developed by Tobibur Rahman. Your privacy is important. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Information We Collect

**Journey does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

All content you create in the app — including journal entries, streaks, and settings — is stored **locally on your device** only. Nothing is sent to any server, and the app does not require an account.

## Permissions

The app requests the following permissions, used solely for on-device functionality:

- **Biometric (fingerprint/face):** to optionally lock the app. Biometric data is handled by your device's operating system and is never accessed by or shared with the app.
- **Notifications:** to deliver the daily journaling reminder you set.
- **Storage (Android 9 and below):** to save PDF or JSON exports of your journal that you explicitly request.

## Data Export and Deletion

- You can export your entire journal to PDF or JSON at any time from the Settings screen.
- You can permanently delete all journal data using "Clear All Data" in Settings. This action cannot be undone.
- Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device.

## Third-Party Services

Journey does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
